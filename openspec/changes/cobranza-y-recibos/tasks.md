# Tasks: cobranza y recibos (slice A: tenant payment and recibo)

Turns `design.md` section 13 (six slices) into ordered, checkable work. It does not re-plan the change
and does not edit `spec.md` or `design.md`. Where slicing exposed a gap, it is listed under
**Issues found** at the end.

Domain vocabulary: **recibo** = receipt (non-fiscal, one per copy); **locatario** = tenant;
**propietario** = lessor/owner; **recargo** = late-payment surcharge; **mora** = being late;
**cutover** = the go-live date and number from which the system, not the spreadsheet, is the record.

- [x] 6.15 **Deadlock between lazy accrual and early payment with pooling on** (no model change, no migration)
  - **Cause:** EF orders same-table INSERTs by key (random Guids), not insertion order. `CollectionDesk.CommitAsync` writes {rent, instalment, payment} and `AccountMaterialiser` {rent, instalment}; the differing batches took `ux_account_movements_period_charge` and `ux_account_movements_instalment_period` in opposite orders, so the sessions deadlocked (`40P01`) about half the time under pooling.

FOR UPDATE` was tried and refused with 42501, the app role has no UPDATE on `contract_accounts`); the materialiser now runs in a transaction (joins the caller's if any) and treats `40P01` like `23505` as a lost race. A losing payer now sees `PreviewStale` (accrual already committed) or `ConcurrentChange`; Tests 70 and the two-payers test accept either (barrier moved to the lock).
  - **Evidence:** new `AnEarlyPayment_RacingTheLazyAccrual_WithPoolingOn_NeverDeadlocks` (20 rounds, pooled sessions): RED 3/3 with `40P01` before the fix; GREEN 10/10 runs after it (Test 70 and the pooled variant).
